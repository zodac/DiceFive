DiceFive is a classic five-dice scorecard game. Roll up to three times a turn, hold the dice you want to keep, and decide where each roll scores. Chase a full house, a large straight or a 5x, and try to make the upper-section bonus before your boxes run out.

Play solo to beat your best score, pass the phone around with up to four players, or fill the empty seats with computer opponents on Easy, Medium or Hard.

SEVEN WAYS TO PLAY
• Standard - the classic rules: 13 boxes, three rolls a turn, a 35-point upper bonus and 100-point bonuses for every extra 5x.
• Tricolour - every die also lands red, yellow or blue, and four colour boxes join the card. Five of one colour, or a coloured full house, opens up new ways to score.
• Quickfire - a six-turn game: the 5x box and six random boxes are switched off, and the upper bonus shrinks to match.
• 7 Dice Stud - roll seven dice, but only the five you hold can score.
• Third Wind - every box is scored three times, with a bigger upper bonus to match.
• D20 - twenty-sided dice and five rolls a turn, with a card of the numbers 1 to 20 and a bonus for each half.
• Hit List - a fresh list of dice targets every game. Roll a target's numbers to hit it, or land them in order for double.

HOUSE RULES
Mix in optional modifiers with any mode: a turn timer, more or fewer rolls a turn, unused rolls that carry over, extra boxes for Two Pair, Evens and Odds, and unlucky dice that lock in place.

GOOGLE PLAY GAMES
DiceFive needs no account and no internet connection, and your games stay on your device. If you like, sign in to Google Play Games from Settings:
• Achievements - your unlocks appear in Play Games, including ones earned before you signed in
• Global leaderboards - one for each mode, to see how your best scores compare
• Cloud save - your achievements, scores, styles and game in progress follow you to your other devices
Sign out at any time, and the game carries on offline as before.
