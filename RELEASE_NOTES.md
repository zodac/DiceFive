## Achievements

A bit of a rework of achievements this release, which has removed/changed some existing achievements. The total count has not changed.

- Replaced `Third Times The Charm` achievement with `Chance Encounter`
- Updated the logic for `I Can Count!` achievement to require scoring 1-2-3-4-5, not rolling it on the first turn
- Removed `Empty House` and `Fuller House` achievements and replaced it with `Full Houses`
- Replaced `Naturally Gifted` achievement with `Slow Start`
- Reordered and recategorised some achievements
- Changed the icons of some achievements
