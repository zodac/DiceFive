DiceFive can now be played in other languages: Spanish and Arabic are new, with Arabic laid out right to left. The app follows your phone's language (or the per-app language setting on Android 13+), and falls back to English for anything else.

- Every screen, spoken (TalkBack) text, number, date and plural form is translated, not just the labels
- Arabic uses Arabic-Indic numerals and a mirrored layout
- Sentences read naturally in each language, with no half-translated screens
