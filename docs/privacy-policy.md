# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2 October 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how DiceFive ("the App") handles information when you use the App.

## 1. Information We Collect

DiceFive is a dice game and does not use advertising or analytics services.

The App does not directly collect or require you to provide personal information such as your name,
email address, postal address, telephone number, or payment information.

The App lets you type in names for the players in a game. These names are stored only on your device
(see section 4) and are not sent to us.

The App may use Google Play Games Services to provide features such as player accounts, achievements,
and other Google Play Games functionality. When you use these features, Google may process information
associated with your Google Play Games account in accordance with Google's own privacy policies.

The App may also use cloud storage or cloud-save functionality to synchronise your game progress and
settings between devices. This information may be associated with your Google Play Games account or
another account or service used to provide the cloud-save functionality.

## 2. Google Play Services

The App uses Google Play Games Services for features including:

- Player account/sign-in functionality
- Achievements
- Synchronisation of game-related information between devices, where supported

Google may collect and process information when you use Google Play Games Services. Google's handling
of this information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and the applicable Google Play terms and
policies.

For more information, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy:
<https://policies.google.com/privacy>

## 3. Cloud-Saved Game Data

The App may store game-related information using an online cloud-storage or cloud-save service. This
may include information such as:

- Game progress
- Scores and statistics
- Achievements or achievement-related information
- Game settings and preferences

Cloud-saved information is used to allow you to restore or synchronise your game data across
supported devices.

The specific cloud-storage technology used by the App may change as the App develops. Any third-party
service used to provide this functionality will process information according to its own privacy
policy and applicable terms.

## 4. Information Stored on Your Device

The App may store game data, settings, preferences, player names, and other information locally on
your device.

This information is used to operate the game and provide its features.

If you have enabled Android's backup feature on your device, Android may include the App's locally
stored data in your device backup (for example, to your Google account). This backup is managed by
Android and Google under your device and account settings, not by the App, and you can turn it off in
your device's settings.

## 5. Advertising and Analytics

The App does not currently use advertising services or third-party analytics services.

We do not use Google AdMob or similar advertising SDKs.

We do not use analytics services to track how individual users interact with the App.

If the App crashes or stops responding, Google Play may collect technical diagnostic information (such
as crash reports) and share it with us in a form that does not identify you, as described in Google's
Privacy Policy. This depends on your device's settings.

If these practices change in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

## 6. Purchases

The App may offer optional purchases through Google Play Billing in the future.

If purchases are introduced, payments will be processed through Google Play. The App does not receive
or store your complete payment card or other payment credentials.

Google may collect and process information associated with purchases in accordance with Google's
applicable terms and privacy policies.

This Privacy Policy will be updated if necessary when purchasing functionality is introduced.

## 7. Third-Party Services

The App relies on third-party services to provide certain functionality, including Google Play
services.

These services may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies. We
recommend reviewing the privacy policies of these services for more information about how they handle
data.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Because the App may use Google Play Games Services and other Google services, the availability and
operation of certain features may be subject to Google's applicable age requirements and policies.

## 9. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to avoid unnecessary collection of personal information and to use
third-party services in accordance with their intended purposes.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time, particularly when new features or third-party
services are introduced.

The updated policy will be made available at the same URL as this Privacy Policy, and the "Last
updated" date will be changed accordingly.
