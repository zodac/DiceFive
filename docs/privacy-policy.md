# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 23 October 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how DiceFive ("the App") handles information when you use the App.

## 1. Information We Collect

DiceFive is a dice game and does not use advertising or analytics services.

The App does not directly collect or require you to provide personal information such as your name,
email address, postal address, telephone number, or payment information. Its one optional purchase is paid for
through Google Play, which handles the payment (see section 6).

The App lets you type in names for the players in a game. These names are stored on your device (see
section 4) and are not sent to us. If you sign in to Google Play Games, they are also kept in your cloud-saved game data
(see section 3), with your scores, so your other signed-in devices can show them.

Signing in to Google Play Games is optional. The App never signs you in by itself: it does so only when you choose
Sign in in the App's Settings. Until then, nothing leaves your device through the App. The App connects to Google
Play Games only through the Google Play services app on your device, and only when it was installed from Google Play.
Copies of the App downloaded from elsewhere never use Google Play Games.

## 2. Google Play Games Services

While you are signed in, the App uses Google Play Games Services for:

- **Your Play Games account.** The App shows your Play Games gamer name and avatar in its Settings.
- **Achievements.** Achievements you unlock in the App are also unlocked on your Play Games account, and those
  already on your account are shown in the App.
- **Leaderboards.** Your final score from each game you play as the first player, under a game mode's standard rules,
  is sent to that mode's Google Play Games leaderboard. Leaderboards are public: other players can see your score
  with your gamer name and avatar, as your Play Games profile settings allow. The player names you type into the App
  are not sent to leaderboards.
- **Saved games.** Your game data is kept in your Google account's Play Games saved games (see section 3).

Google may also collect information when you use Google Play Games Services, such as diagnostic and usage
information about the service itself. Google's handling of this information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy
and the applicable Google Play terms and policies.

For more information, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy:
<https://policies.google.com/privacy>

## 3. Cloud-Saved Game Data

While you are signed in to Google Play Games, the App keeps a copy of your game data in your Play Games saved games,
so that your devices signed in to the same account share it. It includes:

- Scores and statistics, with the player names typed in for them
- Achievements and achievement progress
- Game settings and preferences, including the styles you have chosen
- The game in progress, if there is one

This data is stored by Google in your Google account. It is not sent to us, and we cannot access it.

Signing out of Google Play Games in the App stops this syncing, but does not delete the copy already saved. Resetting
the Leaderboard in the App also clears the scores in the saved copy. You can delete the App's saved games, and all
other Play Games data for the App, from your Play Games profile; you can delete your whole Play Games account and its
data from your Play Games profile or your Google Account. Scores already on a leaderboard and achievements already
unlocked on your Play Games account are kept by Google Play Games, and the App cannot remove them.

## 4. Information Stored on Your Device

The App may store game data, settings, preferences, player names, and other information locally on
your device.

This information is used to operate the game and provide its features.

If you have enabled Android's backup feature on your device, Android may include the App's locally
stored data in your device backup (for example, to your Google account). This backup is managed by
Android and Google under your device and account settings, not by the App, and you can turn it off in
your device's settings.

## 5. Advertising and Analytics

The App does not show advertising, and does not use advertising services or third-party analytics services.

We do not use Google AdMob or similar advertising SDKs.

We do not use analytics services to track how individual users interact with the App.

If the App crashes or stops responding, Google Play may collect technical diagnostic information (such
as crash reports) and share it with us in a form that does not identify you, as described in Google's
Privacy Policy. This depends on your device's settings.

If these practices change in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

## 6. Purchases

The App offers one optional, one-time purchase, "Unlock all styles", which unlocks every table style straight away.
Every style can still be earned for free by playing. The purchase is offered only in copies of the App installed from
Google Play; copies downloaded from elsewhere offer no purchases.

- **Payment.** The purchase is made through Google Play Billing, on Google Play's own purchase screen. Google Play
  processes the payment. The App never receives or stores your payment card, other payment details, name, address or
  email address, and none of it is sent to us.
- **What the App receives.** From the Google Play account signed in to the Play Store on your device, the App learns
  only whether that account owns the purchase, and confirms the purchase back to Google Play so that it is completed.
  It asks when it starts and when you return to it, so that a purchase you made on another device, or one that
  finishes later (a pending payment), is recognised.
- **What the App stores.** One setting on your device recording that every style is unlocked (see section 4). It is
  not part of your cloud-saved game data, and it is not sent to us.
- **Refunds and restoring.** If a purchase is refunded or cancelled, the App sees that the account no longer owns it,
  and the styles you haven't earned lock again; the styles you picked are kept, ready for when you earn them. If you
  reinstall the App, or use another device with the same Google Play account, the purchase is restored automatically.
- **Google.** Google collects and processes information about purchases, such as your order history, under the Google
  Play Terms of Service, the Google Payments Privacy Notice, and Google's Privacy Policy
  (<https://policies.google.com/privacy>). You can see and manage your Google Play orders in your Google account.

## 7. Third-Party Services

The App relies on third-party services to provide certain functionality, including Google Play
services.

These services may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies. We
recommend reviewing the privacy policies of these services for more information about how they handle
data.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

The App's purchase is made through Google Play, so Google Play's parental controls and Google Family Link purchase
approvals apply to it.

Because the App may use Google Play Games Services and other Google services, the availability and
operation of certain features may be subject to Google's applicable age requirements and policies.

## 9. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to avoid unnecessary collection of personal information and to use
third-party services in accordance with their intended purposes.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time, particularly when new features or third-party
services are introduced.

The updated policy will be made available at the same URL as this Privacy Policy, and the "Last
updated" date will be changed accordingly.
